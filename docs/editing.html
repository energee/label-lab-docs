Editing

Selecting

Click a layer on the preview or in the list. Click the empty pasteboard around the sheet, or the inspector's ✕, to clear it (inspector closes too).

  • ⇧-click extends
  • ⌘-click / Ctrl-click toggles
  • Multi-select draws a group frame around the layers

Typing

Double-click a text layer on the preview and you edit in place, at print metrics, pill toolbar floating above. The sidebar is the same editor.

Esc or a click away finishes the edit and keeps what you typed, as in Figma or Illustrator. ⌘Z takes the whole edit back in one step.

  • Header: bigger line (heading scale in the inspector)
  • Bold
  • White ink underlay: that line (or run) goes on the white-ink plate. Fill colour stays. Colour and plate otherwise come from the inspector's Fill and Metallic, which restyle every line at once; a line or run you gave the underlay keeps it
  • Uppercase: whole layer, without losing what you actually typed. On the pill only; in the sidebar it is the inspector's Typography Uppercase switch

Select part of a line and hit Bold or White ink underlay to wrap just that span. Type **bold** or _white ink_ and it becomes a run. The markers vanish. A run can span several words (**GOVERNMENT WARNING:**) and keeps its style when the line wraps or justifies. There is no escape hatch for a literal pair.

In the sidebar, ⇧Tab from a text field reaches its toolbar. The buttons act on the selection you left in the field.

Number and code layers, and any layer with a choice list (Style), get a plain box, on the canvas and in the sidebar alike. A new value typed into a choice field joins the master's list once, when you press Enter or Esc or click away, never letter by letter.

Number and code boxes validate. On the canvas, what you type is kept as typed. A value that isn't a number, is out of range, or isn't a valid code shows its error under the box (on the canvas, above it when the box sits near the bottom of the label) and blocks export until you fix it. A number box takes decimals such as 5.8, and in the sidebar the arrow keys keep the fraction.

Moving and resizing

Pointer down on an unlocked layer, drag a few pixels, and it (or the whole selection) moves. Corner and edge ticks on the outline resize.

While you drag or resize:

  • Layers snap to other layers' edges and middles, and to page guides (including ones you pulled off the rulers)
  • Fuchsia lines are another layer. Blue lines are a page guide
  • A badge reads how far you travelled, or the width and height while resizing, in the unit the rulers use (View → Units)

Arrow keys nudge 1 point. Shift makes it 10. They stay out of the way while you're typing or a menu is open.

Locked layers don't move on the canvas. Powered-off layers don't draw and you can't click them.

Undo

⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z (or ⌘Y) cover the document: fills, style, position, structure. Edits within half a second collapse into one step, so a scrub undoes as a single move. Typing in one inspector field is one step of its own, however slowly you type, and never merges with the edit before or after it. So is an on-preview text edit, from double-click until you leave it.

Loaded texture and mask files are not on that stack. Stepping back never re-decodes an image. Removing one with its well's × leaves a note with its own Undo, until the slot is filled again. An image layer's art is on the stack: the note under its well points to ⌘Z. While a text field has focus, that field's own undo wins.

What you're actually changing

Moving a name, restyling gold, typing this beer: that's the label. Promote those overrides in Save Master when they should become the template. Adding a layer or a variant is already a master edit. It will be on the review.
